# Complete cleaned lecture records

Use this mode when the user wants the meeting's actual teaching content, not a thematic summary and not a mechanical verbatim transcript.

## Reader body

- Keep the speaker's course path, reasoning, actions, conditions, numbers, cases, objections and effective questions.
- Keep original first-person or spoken reasoning where it carries meaning. Do not replace a multi-step explanation with “the speaker introduced” or “the course emphasized”.
- Remove stutters, repeated filler, identical adjacent restatements, applause, chants, equipment adjustment, photography reminders, breaks, logistics, pricing, promotion and private chatter.
- Merge adjacent speech only when no reason, condition, example, contrast or deliberate emphasis is lost. Keep useful student questions in a reader-facing callout; use only confirmed identities.
- Do not display timestamps, input IDs, evidence labels or hash data in the reader body. Keep precise time coverage, speaker uncertainty and exclusions in the sidecar.

## Speech correction

Resolve a recognition error only in this order:

1. Explicit slide or document text;
2. The same term repeated clearly in the selected session;
3. A single unambiguous local context;
4. A standard proper noun or term supported by the selected material.

Correct confirmed proper nouns, terms, numbers and sentence boundaries. If two reasonable readings remain, keep [听辨存疑：原词]; do not use outside knowledge to choose one. Record only meaning-changing corrections in the sidecar:

正文块｜原转写片段｜修复结果｜依据｜确定性

Do not turn a speaker claim, case result or disputed fact into an editorial fact while correcting transcription.

## Acceptance

Pass the existing learning_check only when the body can be followed as a lecture without reopening the transcript. Pass anti_summary_check only when the cleanup has not removed a distinct argument, step, number, case boundary, question, objection or failure. Keep the source partial while this check is incomplete.
