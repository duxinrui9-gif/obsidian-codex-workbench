# Maturity contract

Knowledge cards require `maturity`, `confidence`, `source_notes`, `source_count`, `review_due`, `contradicts`, and `supersedes`. New knowledge uses `knowledge_kind`, `operational_readiness`, `direct-content-v4`, `evidence_families`, `independent_source_count`, and `applied_validation_count`. Active methods additionally require `evidence-bound-method-v4`. Existing v2/v3 records remain readable legacy material and are not batch rewritten.

Use these maturity meanings:

- `seed`: idea or one unverified source/thread.
- `candidate`: a principle, case, or method fragment has a scoped claim and at least one verified direct v4 evidence family; a method additionally has its complete v4 E/D contract and no missing core action.
- `validated`: candidate plus two independent evidence families, or one direct family plus one complete `applied_validation` source with an explicit result.
- `evergreen`: two independent families, two cross-context applied validations, a current review, and no unresolved core conflict.
- `stale` or `superseded`: retain the record, state the newer evidence, and use `review` or `archived` status.

Do not infer independence from multiple formats of one event. Indexes, sidecar manifests, summaries, and repeated formats from the same event do not increase maturity. For a `direct-content-v4` card, first verify that every cited source is `semantic-content-v4 / content / complete / coverage_verified: true` with applicable gates passed and usable fidelity: PDF evidence must have concrete element blocks and page-specific visual descriptions; a `slide-photo-primary-learning-v1` source additionally needs a valid photo sidecar with every image transcribed, duplicated, excluded or explicitly unreadable; any learning source needs accepted learning and anti-summary gates; meeting evidence must have precise timestamp-topic blocks, not a whole-session compatibility anchor. Otherwise record an evidence gap or lower the card rather than promoting it. Preserve conflicts and record the evidence gap rather than selecting a preferred conclusion.

For a photo-specific claim, require the visual transcription plus its exact `^t-*` reader mapping; a chapter-only mapping or unresolved image-wall finding is insufficient for promotion. Reader rearrangement, visual placement, export styling and de-noising improve traceability only. When a source recompile changes a cited term, number, case boundary, evidence nature or stable-block meaning, review the affected E items before promotion; keep the card and maturity unchanged until the user explicitly applies an audit conclusion.

`maturity` measures evidence strength; `operational_readiness` measures whether a person can execute the documented task. `runnable` may be `seed`; it does not upgrade maturity. `tested` requires at least one complete `applied_validation` source with a stated result. For a v4 card, every cited source must be `semantic-content-v4 / content / complete / coverage_verified: true` with applicable coverage, semantic and visual gates passed; a cited full PDF additionally needs `content_fidelity: full` and `fidelity_check: passed`. Full transcription, learning-oriented reorganization and extra visual crops only improve traceability: they do not add evidence families, prove causality, or raise maturity. PDF crops, screenshots and transcripts from one event remain one family.

Project-page observations count as applied validation only after they state an observed result and have been compiled into a hash-traceable v4 source. Keep active canonical methods in `02_Knowledge/方法` and retained superseded cards in `02_Knowledge/归档/方法`. Default mode reports only; an explicit `更新成熟度`、`归档` or `应用审计结论` request is required before modifying a card. A pure redirect duplicate may be deleted only with explicit user authorization, a Vault-external backup, an entry in a replacement/withdrawal record, and no remaining non-project inbound links.
