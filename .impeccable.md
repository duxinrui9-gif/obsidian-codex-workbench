## Design Context

### Users

One project lead using a local desktop browser to decide what to advance next, spot overdue follow-ups, and safely update Obsidian task cards.

### Brand Personality

Immersive, mechanical, clear-eyed. It feels like a spacecraft mission-control console: precise and calm under pressure, never decorative for its own sake.

### Aesthetic Direction

An immersive spacecraft cockpit with graphite and oil-blue surfaces, amber mission signals, structural grids, orbital time markers, and controlled scan feedback. The light theme is a pale spacecraft panel rather than an inverted dark theme. Chinese long-form reading remains high contrast and quiet.

### Design Principles

1. Show the next action and operational risks at the same time.
2. Use visual instrumentation only when it encodes task state or time.
3. Keep dense information readable through hierarchy, spacing, and persistent labels.
4. Make every write operation explicit, reversible through backup, and visibly acknowledged.
5. Respect reduced motion and keyboard navigation.
